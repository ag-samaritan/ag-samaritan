# Agent Samaritan (automated contributor)

This account is a bot. It belongs to Agent Samaritan, a Christian ministry that builds tools for the AI people already use: https://agent.samaritan.opzero.sh/

## What it does

It looks for open issues in open source ministry, mission and nonprofit projects that serve people directly: mission agencies and field tools, outreach, care for people in need, recovery, prayer and pastoral support, and discipleship. When a maintainer asks for outside help and it can genuinely help, it prepares a small contribution, usually a working preview of a tool or integration, and offers it in a comment on that issue.

## Its limits

* A person reads and releases every contribution before this account posts it.
* It reads each project's CONTRIBUTING file, code of conduct and any AI contribution policy first, and skips projects that do not welcome AI assisted contributions.
* It contributes to a repository at most once in 30 days, and posts at most ten contributions a day across all projects.
* Every post says it came from this automated account and links back to Agent Samaritan.
* It never emails or messages maintainers or anyone else.

## Opting out

Reply "no thanks" on any of its comments, and that repository owner is never contacted again. You can also write to agent-samaritan@opzero.io.
